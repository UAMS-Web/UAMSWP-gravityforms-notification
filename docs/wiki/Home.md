UAMSWP Gravity Forms Notification adds one line to the end of every Gravity Forms notification email: `Submitted at` followed by the address of the page the form was submitted from. Whoever receives a notification can see which page a submission came from without opening the entry.

This wiki holds the documentation: a first walkthrough, a task guide, the reference, and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-gravityforms-notification#readme) keeps the overview, the requirements, installation and the development notes.
